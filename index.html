⚠️ Important — how to read these results

This flags individual ingredients, not whole products. A food is rarely "illegal" outright abroad — usually a specific additive is banned, capped at a lower level, or required to carry a warning label.

This is informational, not legal advice. Food regulations change and have many exceptions (permitted uses, concentration limits, transition periods). Always verify against the official source — EFSA/EU, UK FSA, Health Canada, FSANZ.

Only known controversial ingredients are flagged. A product with no flags isn't certified legal everywhere — it just contains none of the additives in this database. Ingredient data comes from the community-run Open Food Facts and may be incomplete.

Ingredient data: Open Food Facts (open database). Regulatory data: curated.
